# Context: Tax-Planner

## Purpose
A modern, lightweight, privacy-focused tax planning tool for Thai Personal Income Tax (ภ.ง.ด. 90/91) tax year 2569 (2026).
Designed for colleagues, friends, and general users to easily model employment income, salary adjustments across companies, PVD, RMF, and Thai ESG deductions with zero friction.

## Key Features
- **Employment Period Selection**: Dropdowns for start and end months (Jan-Dec) without manual 12-month grid clicking.
- **Mid-year Salary Adjustments**: Simple "+ เพิ่มการปรับเงินเดือน" step inputs with visual timeline bar and carry-forward calculation.
- **Accurate PVD & SS**: Dynamically calculates Social Security cap and PVD employee contributions based on actual monthly salary steps.
- **2026 Deductions**: Personal, family, health/life insurance, retirement funds (RMF/PVD/GPF), Thai ESG (300k separate cap), Easy E-Receipt, and donations.
- **Live Settlement & Simulator**: Real-time tax refund/payable hero card and what-if sliders to see tax savings from extra investments.
